A deadpan broadcast forecast for Brindlewick, a city that does not exist: light rain begins at noon and falls upwards after 3 p.m., so residents are advised to hold their umbrellas upside down until 6 p.m. sharp. Running `main.ts` writes that single forecast line to `/output/entry.txt`.
